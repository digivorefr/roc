# Web interface — medium sheet

The sheet of the web medium, the only one that ships with the skill. Its shape is the one every medium sheet follows ([formats.md](formats.md)); its rules are the frame and the catalog, kept in their own files.

## Medium

web interface · shipped with the skill; the marks of its rules hold as written

## Rules

- **The frame** — [frame.md](frame.md), next to this sheet: the rules of web-interface craft in eight families. Each rule carries its title, the decision to take, how its breach shows on the rendered screen, its source, and its mark `firm` or `default`. A rule is cited by its exact title.
- **The catalog** — [catalog.md](catalog.md), next to this sheet: ways to solve interface problems, grouped by the problem the person faces. It holds no rule of its own; its "Avoid when" cites frame rules by their title. The maker and the critic read it; an intent worker never does.

## Material

- A key image and a prototype are built from self-contained HTML, CSS and JavaScript with no network dependency, or from the project's real design-system components shown through its own preview tooling, as the run's `MATERIAL` says.
- Fallback: self-contained HTML when design-system material fails to open, with the reason stated.
- A prototype is throwaway: it proves the design and never ships.

## Show

- A key image and a prototype are opened in the session's browser tooling, or given by path when the session has none.
- Design-system material is shown at the address or story the preview recipe gives.

## Judge

Proof instruments:

- Operating the rendered screen: tabbing through it, triggering every action, reaching every state, narrowing the window, turning on reduced motion.
- Captures of each state, and contrast sampled on them.
- Durations and easings as declared in the code.
- A recording or sampled frames of a transition, when a tool on the bench makes them.

Stays with the operator: whether the intent lands; how a transition feels when nothing recorded it; any sound the interface plays.

## Deliverable

- Founding: `design/language.md`.
- Declining: `design/proposals/<slug>.md` with its captures in `design/captures/<slug>/`.
- Critique and fidelity: findings at the checkpoint and remarks in the corpus; no file in the project.

## Raw materials

Usual: a logo, a typeface, icons, an illustration, a photograph, a background texture, a sound. A logo and a typeface carry identity; the others are accessory.

## Piece types and aspects

The closed list in the corpus's `types.md`: every type and aspect tied to `web`, and every one recorded before media existed. A piece type of this medium is a screen type.
